*Friends, Lovers, and the Big Terrible Thing* is Matthew Perry's memoir,
published in 2022, a year before his death. He writes openly about growing up,
his years on *Friends*, and his decades-long struggle with addiction, with the
same humour he was known for, even when the subject is hard.
