A model-based reinforcement learning agent, inspired by **DreamerV2**, that
learns to drive in urban areas using a simulator.

On top of the agent, I designed and implemented a **continual learning**
framework, so the system keeps improving over time and adapts its behaviour to
the driver's, making the ride more comfortable.
