If you're curious about AI and want to meet others who are too, VAM! might be
worth checking out.

**VAM!**, or Vancouver AI Meetup, is a community I've been attending to learn
more about AI and machine learning. It brings together researchers, engineers,
founders, and students to share ideas and learn from one another. It's organized
by **Issam Laradji**, an adjunct professor at UBC.

The meetups often include talks and reading groups, with time to socialize
afterward. I've enjoyed hearing researchers from UBC and SFU share their work.
It's been a nice way to learn something new and meet people in Vancouver with
similar interests.
