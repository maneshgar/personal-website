**VAM!** (Vancouver AI Meetup) is a community I'm learning a lot from. It's
organized by **Issam Laradji**, an adjunct professor at UBC, and brings together
researchers, engineers, founders and students who want to learn, share ideas and
keep up with what's happening in AI.

I totally recommend it if you want to:

- **Learn more about the field**, through talks, reading groups and hands-on sessions.
- **Connect with people in your community** who are curious about the same things.

Thanks to his network and reputation, Issam is able to bring great figures from
UBC and SFU to share their knowledge with the community, which makes every event
worth attending.
