*Notes on Being a Man* by Scott Galloway, the NYU marketing professor and
podcaster, is written as a practical guide for men today: on work, relationships,
fatherhood and finding a sense of purpose. For me, though, its biggest impact was
getting to know the author himself and his view on life.

Galloway draws heavily on his own story to make his points, and he is
refreshingly candid about it. A few of the ideas that run through the book:

- **Action reduces anxiety.** Doing something, even something small, beats
  worrying about it.
- **Find your talent and follow it**, rather than waiting for a passion to appear.
- **Get out of the house and take risks**, and accept that feeling like an
  imposter is part of growing.
- **Be good to the people around you**, especially as a partner and a father.

You don't have to agree with every point to get something out of it. It's an
honest look at how one person thinks about living a good life.
