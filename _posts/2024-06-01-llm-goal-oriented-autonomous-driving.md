An end-to-end, goal-oriented approach to autonomous driving using large language
models.

We integrated LLMs into the transformer-based **feature extraction** and
**motion estimation** steps of an end-to-end autonomous driving pipeline
(UniAD). The language model provides the driving goal and guides the network's
attention toward the important objects in the scene.
