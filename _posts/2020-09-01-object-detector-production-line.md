An efficient 2D object detector for inspecting products on the production line.

- **Detectors:** I designed and implemented a family of RetinaNet-like
  detectors, with variations trading off speed and resolution.
- **Text reading:** the detectors were adapted to OCR solutions such as EasyOCR
  and EAST, performing detection and recognition in two steps.
