tags: [projects, ai]
description: "Predicting where pedestrians will go, modelling both individual and social behaviour."
project_where: Huawei Technologies Canada
project_period: 2021
---

Predicting the trajectories of multiple pedestrians from the point of view of
the ego vehicle, using a cascaded **coarse-to-fine** approach:

- **Human behaviour** is modelled with generative models (a conditional VAE).
- **Social behaviour and interactions** between pedestrians are modelled with
  graph convolutional networks and attention.
