I recently started MIT's **Finance Theory I** (15.401), a full university course
that MIT OpenCourseWare shares for free on YouTube. I'm in the middle of it now,
and I'm genuinely excited about it, so I wanted to share it with you.

The course builds up the fundamentals of finance step by step: how to value
money over time, bonds and stocks, risk and return, building a portfolio, and
how markets price all of it. Whether you're curious about investing or just want
to understand money better, it's a great place to start.

If you've ever wanted to learn finance properly, give the first lecture a try:
