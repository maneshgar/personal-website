**GeneExpert** is my first introduction to bioinformatics, and my first PhD project.

Bulk RNA-Seq is one of the most widely used ways to measure gene expression, in
both research and clinical settings. Yet while foundation models have
transformed how we learn from data like protein sequences, single-cell data and
medical images, bulk RNA-Seq has been left largely unexplored.

GeneExpert is our step toward closing that gap: a foundation model that learns
general-purpose representations of gene expression, capturing information about
both samples and genes. The goal is representations that are biologically
meaningful and useful across a wide range of biological and clinical
applications.

Alongside the model, we're also building a way to systematically evaluate
foundation models for transcriptomics.

The results so far are very encouraging. More details will follow once the work
is published, so stay tuned!
